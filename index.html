About the Bartender
A note from T

I’m terrible at remembering recipes. Always have been. But I also always want to make something new — and I’m forever limited by whatever is actually sitting on my shelf.

My bar holds the usual bottles — my usual, anyway: vodka, gin, tequila, and rum, alongside the normal mixers and fruit juices. One night I took stock of my inventory, top to bottom, and then went researching every cocktail I could actually make with what I had.

The results were amazing. Dozens of drinks — classics, forgotten gems, tiki birds — hiding in a cabinet I looked at every day. And I thought: who wouldn’t want this on their phone?

So, here it is.

Load my shelf with one tap — that’s the “T’s House” preset in My Bar — or clear it out and check off your own liquors, mixers, and garnishes. The menu rebuilds itself around whatever you’ve got, shows you what you can pour tonight, and tells you which single bottle unlocks the most new drinks next.

Cheers,
— T
